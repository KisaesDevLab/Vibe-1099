Instructions for Recipient — Form 1099-DIV

Recipient's TIN. For your protection, this form may show only the last four digits of your TIN. However, the payer has reported your complete TIN to the IRS.

Box 1a.

Shows total ordinary dividends that are taxable. Include this amount on line 3b of Form 1040 or 1040-SR. Also report it on Schedule B (Form 1040), if required.

Box 1b.

Shows the portion of the amount in box 1a that may be eligible for reduced capital gains rates (qualified dividends).

Box 2a.

Shows total capital gain distributions from a regulated investment company (RIC) or real estate investment trust (REIT). Report on Schedule D (Form 1040) or, if no Schedule D is required, on line 7 of Form 1040 or 1040-SR.

Boxes 2b–2d.

Portions of the amount in box 2a: unrecaptured section 1250 gain, section 1202 gain, and 28% rate (collectibles) gain.

Boxes 2e–2f.

Section 897 amounts — generally applicable only to foreign persons.

Box 3.

Shows a return of capital (nondividend distribution). This is not taxable until it exceeds your basis.

Box 4.

Shows backup withholding. Include this amount on your income tax return as tax withheld.

Box 5.

Shows the portion of the amount in box 1a that may be eligible for the 20% qualified business income deduction under section 199A (section 199A dividends).

Box 7.

Shows foreign tax paid — you may be able to claim this as a deduction or credit.

Boxes 9–10.

Cash and noncash liquidation distributions.

Box 11.

If checked, the payer is reporting to satisfy its FATCA account reporting requirement.

Box 12.

Exempt-interest dividends from a mutual fund or other RIC. Report on line 2a of Form 1040.

Box 13.

Specified private activity bond interest dividends (AMT item).

Boxes 14–16.

State tax withheld reporting boxes.
