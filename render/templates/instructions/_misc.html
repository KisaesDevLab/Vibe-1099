Instructions for Recipient — Form 1099-MISC

Recipient's taxpayer identification number (TIN). For your protection, this form may show only the last four digits of your TIN. However, the payer has reported your complete TIN to the IRS.

Account number. May show an account or other unique number the payer assigned to distinguish your account.

Box 1.

Report rents from real estate on Schedule E (Form 1040). If you provided significant services to the tenant, report on Schedule C.

Box 2.

Report royalties from oil, gas, or mineral properties; copyrights; and patents on Schedule E.

Box 3.

Generally, report this amount on the "Other income" line of Schedule 1 (Form 1040).

Box 4.

Shows backup withholding or withholding on Indian gaming profits. Include this amount on your income tax return as tax withheld.

Box 5.

Shows an amount as your share of all proceeds from the sale of a catch, or the fair market value of a distribution in kind to each crew member of a fishing boat. Report on Schedule C.

Box 6.

For individuals, report on Schedule C.

Box 7.

If checked, consumer products totaling $5,000 or more were sold to you for resale.

Box 8.

Shows substitute payments in lieu of dividends or tax-exempt interest. Report on the "Other income" line of Schedule 1 (Form 1040).

Box 9.

Report crop insurance proceeds on Schedule F (Form 1040).

Box 10.

Shows gross proceeds paid to an attorney in connection with legal services.

Box 11.

Shows the amount of cash you received for the sale of fish if you are in the trade or business of catching fish.

Box 12.

May show current-year deferrals as a nonemployee under a nonqualified deferred compensation (NQDC) plan subject to section 409A.

Box 13.

If checked, the payer is reporting on this Form 1099 to satisfy its chapter 4 account reporting requirement (FATCA).

Boxes 14–15.

Excess golden parachute payments and nonqualified deferred compensation.

Boxes 16–18.

State tax withheld reporting boxes.
