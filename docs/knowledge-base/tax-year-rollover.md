# Creating a new filing year (rollover)

**Where:** Settings → **Advanced** → **Filing years** *(admin)*

Each season you open a new tax year so it appears in every date picker and can accept new data. Prior years stay available for late filings and corrections.

## Filing years, at a glance
- **Enabled years** — the list of years your firm can work in. Shown as badges; the **current** one is highlighted.
- **Default (current) year** — the year preselected on new filings and on the tax-year selectors throughout the app.

## Rolling over
Click **Roll over to <next year>**. This:
1. Enables the new year everywhere (all tax-year dropdowns, form entry, filing, batches, etc.).
2. Makes the new year the **default**.
3. Leaves all existing years enabled.

You can also **change the default year** among the enabled years without adding a new one — handy if you jumped ahead and want to work a prior year for a while.

## What carries forward
- **Payers and recipients are year-independent** — they're already available in the new year; nothing to re-import.
- On the **Form entry** grid, use **Rollforward** to bring a payer's prior-year recipients into the new year so you only key the amounts.
- The **client portal** automatically pre-lists last year's contractors for each payer when you invite them.

## Form definitions
The 1099 box layouts and thresholds are defined per year and extend to new years automatically (e.g. the OBBBA $2,000 NEC/MISC threshold applies to 2026 and later). You can override the warn-only thresholds per year under **Settings → E-file → Federal filing thresholds**.

**Tip:** Roll over at the start of the season. Every year selector across the app updates immediately, and new work defaults to the year you just opened.
