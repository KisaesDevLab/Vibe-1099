# W-9 requests & the W-9 portal

Collect a missing tax ID (and address) from a contractor through a secure link, so you can file an accurate 1099.

---

## W-9 requests (staff)
**Sidebar:** People → **W-9 requests**

**What it's for:** tracking outstanding W-9s and chasing them down.

**Key actions:**
- **Request a W-9** — from here, from a recipient row (People → Recipients → *request*), or a client can trigger one from their portal.
- **Resend / remind** — automatic reminders go out on a schedule (configurable in Settings → Advanced), or send one manually.
- Track status: sent, opened, completed, expired.
- When a W-9 comes back, the contractor's details flow into the **[Recipients vault](recipients.md)** and their W-9 status becomes **on file**.

**With Tax1099:** if your firm files through Tax1099, W-9 requests can also be collected through Zenwork's hosted flow in addition to the built-in portal.

---

## The W-9 portal (what the contractor sees)
**URL:** `/w9/:token` (from the request)

A short, secure form where the contractor provides their legal name, tax classification, address, and TIN, then signs. On submit:
- Their information is saved to the vault.
- Their W-9 status flips to **on file**, clearing the "missing W-9" flag on any forms for them.

**Tip:** Requesting W-9s early — via **bulk W-9** from the [Work Inbox](dashboard-and-inbox.md) or [Fleet operations](fleet-operations.md) — is the single best way to avoid TIN/name rejections at filing time.
