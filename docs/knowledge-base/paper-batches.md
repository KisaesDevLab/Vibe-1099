# Paper batches (Z-fold pressure-seal)

**Sidebar:** Filing & delivery → **Paper batches**

**What it's for:** printing recipient **Copy B** as ready-to-seal mailers. Paper Copy B is **always mailed** — the online portal copy is only a courtesy.

## The Z-fold sheet
Each form prints as **one single-sided sheet** with three panels:
- **Top panel** — the recipient instructions.
- **Fold 1 (middle)** — the substitute 1099 form (Copy B).
- **Fold 2 (bottom)** — the mailer face (the **payer's** return address and the recipient's address; becomes the outside when folded).

Feed the printed sheets through a **pressure sealer**. Only boxes with an entry are printed (blank boxes are omitted), which keeps each form to a single panel.

## Building a batch
1. Pick the **tax year**, a **label** (e.g. "January mailing"), and the **form types**.
2. Build the **payer** working list (search-and-add, or **Unmailed** / **All with accepted forms** quick-adds).
3. Choose **one combined batch** or **one batch per payer**.
4. **Preview what will print** — a per-payer count and total sheets.
5. **Build** — the batch renders in the background and appears in the history below. Forms print in **payer → recipient** order; sheet 1 is a manifest.
6. **Advanced** lets you widen which record statuses are included (default: ready-to-mail).

## Working the history
- **Download** the built PDF and print it.
- **Mark printed** → **Mark delivered** to advance the batch and record paper delivery.
- **Drill in** to a batch to **reprint a single form** (e.g. one that jammed in the sealer).
- **Delete** an **unprinted** batch (building/built/failed) if you built it by mistake — its PDF is removed. Printed/delivered batches can't be deleted (they're part of the mailing record).

## Calibration
Use **Calibration test sheet** to check alignment against your pressure-seal stock. Fine-tune the imposition offsets in **Settings → Firm** (±1/16″ steps) until the fold lines and address window line up.

**Tip:** Use **Unmailed (accepted, no paper sent)** to sweep exactly the forms that still owe a paper copy.
