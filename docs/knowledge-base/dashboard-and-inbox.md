# Dashboard & Work Inbox

These two screens answer "where do things stand?" and "what needs me right now?" across every entity you file for.

---

## Dashboard
**Sidebar:** Season → **Dashboard** (the home screen)

**What it's for:** a control tower for the whole season. It rolls up totals and shows a per-payer progress table with deadline risk.

**What you see:**
- Roll-up counts for the selected **tax year** (entered, ready, transmitted, accepted, rejected, delivered).
- A sortable, filterable **per-payer** table showing each entity's progress through the pipeline.
- **Deadline** dates for the year and which payers are at risk.

**Key actions:**
- **Sort / filter** the table (e.g. show only payers with rejects, unfiled, or undelivered forms).
- **Saved views** — save a filter/sort combination you check often and reload it in one click.
- Click into a payer to drill into its forms, or jump to the **Work Inbox** for the actionable list.

**Tips:**
- Change the **Tax year** selector to review a prior season; the whole page re-rolls.
- Use the "rejects" and "unfiled" filters as your daily punch list near a deadline.

---

## Work Inbox
**Sidebar:** Season → **Work inbox**

**What it's for:** the unified "what needs me" queue across all payers — the single list of open items, paginated and filterable, with one-click jumps to resolve each one.

**What surfaces here:**
- Client submissions waiting for review.
- Forms stuck in draft/ready that need to be advanced.
- Recipients missing a W-9 or a mailing address.
- Rejected forms that need fixing.
- Undelivered accepted forms.

**Key actions:**
- Filter by the kind of work, then click an item to jump straight to the screen that resolves it.
- Kick off **bulk W-9** requests for everyone missing a tax ID.

**Tip:** The Inbox is the fastest way to clear a season — work it top to bottom rather than hunting screen by screen.
