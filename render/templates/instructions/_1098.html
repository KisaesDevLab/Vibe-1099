Instructions for Payer/Borrower — Form 1098

A person (including a financial institution, a governmental unit, and a cooperative housing corporation) who is engaged in a trade or business and, in the course of such trade or business, received from you at least $600 of mortgage interest on any one mortgage during the calendar year must furnish this statement to you.

Payer's/Borrower's TIN. For your protection, this form may show only the last four digits of your TIN. However, the recipient/lender has reported your complete TIN to the IRS.

Box 1.

Shows the mortgage interest received by the recipient/lender during the year. This amount does not include points, government subsidy payments, or seller payments on a "buydown" mortgage. Such amounts are deductible by you only in certain circumstances.

Box 2.

Shows the outstanding principal on the mortgage as of January 1 of the calendar year. If the mortgage originated during the year, it shows the mortgage principal as of the origination date.

Box 3.

Shows the date of the mortgage origination.

Box 4.

Shows any refund of overpaid interest you made. It may reduce your mortgage interest deduction; do not deduct this amount. See the instructions for Schedule A (Form 1040).

Box 5.

Shows the amount of premiums paid for mortgage insurance. This amount may be deductible as qualified residence interest depending on the tax year and current law.

Box 6.

Shows points paid on the purchase of your principal residence that are deductible. Points you paid that are not reported in box 6 may also be deductible.

Box 7.

If checked, the address of the property securing the mortgage is the same as the payer's/borrower's address shown on this statement.

Box 8.

Shows the address or a description of the property securing the mortgage, when it is not the same as the payer's/borrower's address.

Box 9.

If more than one property secures the loan, shows the number of properties securing the mortgage. If only one property secures the loan, this box may be blank.

Box 10.

The interest recipient may use this box to give you other information, such as real estate taxes or insurance paid from escrow.

Box 11.

Shows the date the interest recipient acquired the mortgage.
